PETAL & STEM

Services

Visual Identity

Year

2026

Info

For this project, I developed the brand identity and visual concept for Petal & Stem, an independent florist. The aim was to create a visual identity that feels warm, modern, and connected to nature.The process included defining the brand’s values and personality, focused on authenticity, confidence, and simplicity. I designed the logo and visual system and applied it across different touchpoints, including, business cards, tags, and social media.Together, these elements create a clear and cohesive identity for Petal & Stem, reflecting its natural, personal, and contemporary character.
