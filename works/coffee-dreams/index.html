COFFEE DREAMS

Services

Visual Identity

Year

2026

Info

For this project, I developed the brand identity and visual concept for Coffee Dreams, a coffee store inspired by the idea of coffee as a moment of pause, comfort, and imagination. The brand is built around the emotional connection between coffee and everyday dreams.The process included defining the brand’s values and personality, which focus on warmth, creativity, and authenticity. I designed the logo, posters, and visual system, social media applications, a tote bag and the design of coffee bean packaging, extending the brand experience to physical products ensuring a cohesive and recognizable identity.Together, these elements create a consistent and immersive identity for Coffee Dreams.
